Research Literature Sources & Citations

Published standards, technical committee reports, and landmark papers anchoring the 301 reference mixes across 12 special concrete types.

Literature-Anchored Reference Policy

Every record in the special concrete dataset carries its own peer-reviewed literature citations. Proportions are representative baseline mixes (typical dosages, water-binder ratios, and absolute-volume closure at 1.0 m³), not direct copies of any single laboratory. Trial batching and laboratory verification are mandatory prior to any structural deployment.
