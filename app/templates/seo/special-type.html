4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is {{ type_meta.name }} not calculated directly via IS 10262:2019?

Bureau of Indian Standards IS 10262:2019 governs conventional hydraulic Portland cements (OPC 33/43/53, PPC, PSC) using established water-cement strength curves (Clause 5) and standard coarse aggregate grading tables. Non-conventional binders, chemical activation, internal polymerization, microfibres, and cellular foaming involve completely different rheological and chemical mechanisms not covered by IS 10262. ConcreteMix AI anchors proposals on peer-reviewed literature and verifies physical volume continuity.

What mandatory lab trials must be conducted before batching?

Field deployment mandates trial mixes to establish: (1) Setting time and workability retention, (2) Compatibility between chemical admixtures and local precursors, (3) 7-day, 28-day, and 56-day compressive and flexural strength gain, and (4) Durability indicators (drying shrinkage, rapid chloride permeability, and carbonation resistance).
